SintaChat Terms & Community Agreement

Please read these terms before continuing.

1. Be respectful

Treat every person with respect. Harassment, bullying, threats, hate speech, sexual harassment, and targeted abuse are not allowed.

2. Stay anonymous safely

Do not pressure anyone to reveal their identity. Avoid sharing passwords, home addresses, financial information, government IDs, or other sensitive personal information.

3. Adults only — 18+

SintaChat is intended only for users who are 18 years old or older. By continuing, you confirm that you meet this age requirement.

4. Keep conversations lawful

Do not use SintaChat to facilitate illegal activity, fraud, exploitation, stalking, threats, or distribution of unlawful content.

5. Consent and boundaries matter

Respect another user's boundaries. If someone asks you to stop a topic or interaction, stop. You can end an anonymous conversation at any time.

6. No impersonation or manipulation

Do not impersonate another person, falsely claim authority, or deliberately manipulate users into sharing private information.

7. Moderation and safety

SintaChat may apply safety measures, rate limits, and moderation controls to protect the service and its users. Serious abuse may result in access being restricted.

8. Your responsibility

You are responsible for what you choose to send. Think carefully before sharing information, opening links, or moving a conversation outside SintaChat.
