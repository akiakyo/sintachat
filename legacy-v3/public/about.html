About the project

Conversations without the pressure of identity.

SintaChat is a text-only anonymous conversation platform designed for PUP students while welcoming students from schools across the Philippines. Create a nickname, choose your campus or school, select a matching preference, and talk one-on-one.

Why SintaChat exists

Sometimes starting a conversation is easier when there is less pressure. SintaChat keeps the experience simple so students can focus on the conversation itself.

What other users see

Your nickname, selected gender, and campus can be shown to your match. Your real name, student number, email, and profile photo are not part of the public chat profile.

Safety

Users can report or block a conversation partner. SintaChat is anonymous to other users, but the platform can still keep internal session records needed for moderation.

Independent project

SintaChat is an independent student project and is not an official service of the Polytechnic University of the Philippines.
