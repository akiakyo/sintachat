Terms and community rules

Keep SintaChat respectful and safe.

By using SintaChat, you agree to follow these basic rules for anonymous conversations.

Respect other users

Do not harass, threaten, bully, discriminate against, or deliberately intimidate another person.

Do not expose private information

Do not pressure users to reveal personal details. Avoid sharing passwords, addresses, financial information, student numbers, or other sensitive information.

No spam or abuse

Do not flood conversations, automate messages, impersonate others, or misuse reporting and blocking features.

Moderation

Reports may be reviewed and abusive sessions may be restricted or blocked from the service.

Independence

SintaChat is an independent student project and is not officially affiliated with the Polytechnic University of the Philippines.
